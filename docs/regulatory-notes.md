# Regulatory Notes

Working notes, not legal advice. Flags to resolve before public release.

## Classification

- **Powered wheelchair** — FDA Class II, 21 CFR 890.3860. Applies if the device is marketed as a wheelchair for a person with a disability.
- **Mobility trainer / DIY / toy** — the TMT itself sits outside medical-device regulation by being a published open design, not a marketed product. The powered add-on may or may not inherit that position. **[TBD — decide and document.]**

## Ventilator accommodation

- Mounting a ventilator on the chair is in scope. **Sharing the chair's drive battery with a ventilator is a separate question** — it places the battery in the ventilator's power path and likely pulls it into the ventilator's regulatory envelope.
- Default position until resolved: ventilator uses its own manufacturer-supplied battery. The chair may provide a *mount* and strain relief, not power.
- ⛔ No passengers / buddy riding with a ventilator mounted.

## Liability posture for AI assist

- Advisory and subtract-only by design. Never commands motion or steering.
- Independent of the Safety Floor — a failed assist module must not degrade basic chair safety.
- Document clearly that assist is a convenience, not a safety certification.
